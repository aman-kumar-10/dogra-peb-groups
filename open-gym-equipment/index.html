Frequently Asked Questions

Open Gym & Garden Swing FAQ

Common questions from municipalities, contractors, and public works committees.

How can government departments order equipment through the GeM Portal? +

Dogra PEB Groups is an authorized and verified enterprise on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM). Departments can locate our OEM-listed products, request customized bids, or proceed with direct procurement using our vendor credentials and MSME certification (UDYAM-HP-04-0016272).

Do you provide on-site civil installation, or only material supply? +

We provide complete turnkey services! This includes material manufacturing, nationwide transit, excavation of foundation pits, M20 grade concrete footing casting, anchor bolt grouting, and final stability inspection.

What steel grades and coatings are used for outdoor weather resistance? +

We utilize Class B Mild Steel pipes conforming to IS 1239 / IS 1161 with minimum 3.0mm to 3.6mm wall thickness. Products undergo zinc phosphate chemical anti-rust passivation followed by pure polyester electrostatic powder coating that resists UV fading and monsoons.

Are children's park swings and slides certified safe? +

Yes. All children's park equipment is built with smooth rounded edges, lead-free non-toxic coatings, anti-pinch rubberized chains, slip-resistant ladder rungs, and shock-absorbing base stops.
